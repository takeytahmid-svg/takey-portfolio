FAQ

Common Questions

Do you work with small budgets? +
Yes. I've worked with budgets from a few thousand taka to $78,000+. What matters is having enough budget to generate meaningful data — typically ৳15,000–20,000/month minimum for Meta Ads to function properly.
How long before I see results? +
The learning phase takes 7–14 days before campaigns stabilise. Meaningful optimisation data usually appears in 3–4 weeks. I set realistic expectations from day one — no overnight promises.
Do I need CAPI if I already have the Pixel? +
Yes. The Pixel alone misses 20–40% of conversions due to iOS 14 and ad blockers. CAPI fills that gap. Together they give Meta the clearest possible signal to find your best customers.
What industries do you work with? +
EdTech, medical tourism, and service-based businesses are my strongest verticals. Open to ecommerce and other industries — campaign optimisation fundamentals transfer across niches.
How do you report results? +
Weekly reports covering CPL, CTR, CPM, frequency, ROAS, and lead quality. No vanity metrics. You'll always know exactly how your budget is performing.
